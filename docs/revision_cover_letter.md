# Cover letter to Next Chemical Engineering

4 September 2026

Dear Editor,

Please consider for publication in Next Chemical Engineering our revised manuscript, “Reactive ePC-SAFT Modeling of Carbon Dioxide Absorption in Aqueous MEA,” by Tanner W. Polley and John D. Hedengren. We thank you and the reviewers for the comments, which helped us clarify the thermodynamic formulation, strengthen the presentation of the results and define the contribution more precisely.

The revision explicitly couples five chemical-equilibrium reactions among nine liquid species with ePC-SAFT activities and fugacity. A schematic and model-quantity table distinguish the roles of the equation of state, reaction equilibrium, conventional enhancement and empirical caloric and transport relations. Expanded appendices document the parameters, their sources and the property correlations.

The revised manuscript presents comparison with seven NCCC absorber cases, paired Case 3C numerical refinement, thermodynamic and transport sensitivity studies, operating perturbations and measured computational cost. It focuses on the coupled reactive absorber formulation rather than a comparison of numerical methods. The discussion distinguishes the completed results from proposed extensions to other amines, predictive film modeling and constrained optimization.

We provide a detailed point-by-point response identifying the manuscript changes for each reviewer comment. We have also revised the language, reduced repeated convergence reporting and consolidated detailed numerical diagnostics in Table 11.

Thank you for considering the revised manuscript.

Sincerely,

Tanner W. Polley and John D. Hedengren

Department of Chemical Engineering, Brigham Young University

Corresponding author: John D. Hedengren, john_hedengren@byu.edu
