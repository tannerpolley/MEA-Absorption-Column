    \section*{Data Availability}
The historical six-species benchmark inputs, processed tables and plotted values are identified by the \href{https://github.com/tannerpolley/MEA-Absorption-Column/tree/0d4e552e15fdf9ee17edc64908c3a04a6525f98b}{nine-case repository revision dated 2026-08-25}.
The coupled Case 3C profiles and numerical-verification values are retained separately under \path{analyses/nccc_validation/figures/reactive_column/output}; its \path{sensitivity} subdirectory contains the thermodynamic comparison, and \path{analyses/transport_sensitivity} contains the transport comparison.
The seven-case temperature and capture comparisons are retained under \path{analyses/nccc_validation/figures/reactive_parallel}, and the three-repeat runtime evidence under \path{analyses/nccc_validation/results/runs/runtime_diagnostics_20260904}.
The associated run records identify the evaluated inputs and source-file hashes. These coupled datasets are not contained in the linked August revision and have no public archival identifier stated here.
