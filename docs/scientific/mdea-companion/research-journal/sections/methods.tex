\section{Methods under development}

\subsection{Generic amine integration}

The column accepts one amine contract containing molar mass, liquid physical-property
correlations, chemical-equilibrium behavior, enhancement behavior, and a complete readiness
declaration. The MEA contract retains the established implementation. The MDEA contract is
fail-closed while required inputs remain unadmitted.
