\section{Current limitations and claim boundary}

The repository contains source-backed MDEA property, ideal-speciation, and kinetic scaffolding,
but these pieces do not constitute an admitted reactive thermodynamic model or a validated
absorption-column result. In particular, no claim is made for predictive MDEA VLE, reactive
bubble pressure, derivative quality, absorber capture, or temperature profiles. The frozen
MEA-only manuscript remains the sole submission-ready scientific artifact.
