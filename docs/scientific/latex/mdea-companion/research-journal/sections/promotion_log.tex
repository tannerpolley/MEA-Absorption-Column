\section{Promotion gates and log}\label{sec:promotion-gates}

MDEA material may enter the candidate manuscript only when all of the following are true:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Every admitted pressure row and speciation state evaluates successfully with one frozen
  parameter set; no failed admitted row is removed from the reported denominator.
  \item Full reactive bubble/VLE behavior and reactive speciation pass mass, charge, reaction,
  phase-equilibrium, and domain checks.
  \item Required first derivatives are finite and agree with independent numerical checks over
  the admitted domain.
  \item Engine commit, immutable wheel SHA-256, parameter-artifact identity, datasets, and
  solver configuration are recorded and reproducible.
  \item The installed-wheel integration gate passes without mutable source imports.
  \item MEA regression remains unchanged and the MDEA column cases converge without hidden
  failed cases or solvent-specific constants outside the amine contract.
  \item Capture and temperature-profile comparisons against admitted MDEA column data meet
  predeclared acceptance criteria.
  \item Manuscript language is restricted to the domain and evidence actually validated.
\end{enumerate}
