Definitions:
f = 1e-12, c = 100, d = sqrt(f*c) = 1e-5;
a = f+d, b = c-d, u = w+1.
g(w) = f+d/(1+(a-u)/d) for u<a;
       u for a<=u<=b;
       c-d/(1+(u-b)/d) for u>b.
g'(w) = 1/(1+(a-u)/d)^2 in the lower tail;
        1 in the interior;
        1/(1+(u-b)/d)^2 in the upper tail.
The inverse uses u=a-d*(d/(y-f)-1) below a,
u=y in the interior, and u=b+d*(d/(c-y)-1) above b;
w=u-1.
At each join the values coincide and both first derivatives equal 1.
For every finite real w, the exact derivative is positive and f<g(w)<c.
The inverse is continuous on (f,c).
